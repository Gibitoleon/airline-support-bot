# Airport Lounge Services

Kenya Airways operates premium lounges at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) that provide passengers with a comfortable environment before departure. The lounges offer services designed to improve the airport experience, including dining facilities, relaxation areas, business facilities, and passenger amenities.

## Kenya Airways Lounges

Kenya Airways operates the following lounges:

- Pride Lounge
- Simba Lounge
- Asante Lounge
- Msafiri Lounge

## Lounge Access Eligibility

Passengers eligible for Kenya Airways lounge access include:

- Kenya Airways Business Class passengers.
- SkyTeam Platinum and Gold cardholders.
- Eligible passengers travelling on Kenya Airways partner airlines.
- Economy Class passengers may purchase lounge access subject to availability.

## Lounge Facilities

Kenya Airways lounges provide various facilities including:

- Comfortable seating areas.
- High-speed Wi-Fi access.
- Food and beverage services.
- Buffet meals and snacks.
- Live cooking services at selected lounges.
- Shower facilities.
- Television entertainment.
- Business facilities with workspace areas.
- Charging points for electronic devices.

## Pride Lounge

Pride Lounge is located at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and provides passengers with views of airport operations. The lounge includes business facilities, dining areas, relaxation spaces, and private areas.

## Simba Lounge

Simba Lounge is located at Terminal 1A. It provides passengers with comfortable seating areas, dining facilities, private spaces, and airport views.

A VVIP room is available at Simba Lounge for passengers requiring additional privacy. The room accommodates up to 8 people and steward services are available upon request.

## Asante Lounge

Asante Lounge is located at Terminal 1C and operates 24 hours a day.

Facilities include:

- Buffet dining.
- Live cooking services.
- Snacks and beverages.
- Showers with towels and amenity kits.
- Television entertainment.
- High-speed Wi-Fi.

## Lounge Purchase Options

Passengers travelling in Economy Class may purchase lounge access depending on availability.

Pay Lounge Day Pass rates:

- 0–3 hours: USD 40
- 3–6 hours: USD 75
- 6–9 hours: USD 110
- 9–12 hours: USD 140

Children aged 2–12 years pay 50% of the applicable adult lounge rate.

Infants below 2 years have free lounge access.

## Private Lounge Services

Simba Lounge VIP Room:

- Cost: USD 100 per hour.
- Maximum capacity: 8 people.
- Steward services available upon request.

VVIP Lounge Room:

- Additional privacy service available.
- Cost: USD 50 per hour in addition to Simba Lounge access fee.
- Maximum capacity: 5 people.
- Steward services available upon request.

## Lounge Conditions

Lounge access is subject to:

- Space availability.
- Lounge admission policies.
- Applicable terms and conditions.

Once a lounge service has been purchased, it cannot be refunded.

Pay Lounge Day Passes are non-transferable.
