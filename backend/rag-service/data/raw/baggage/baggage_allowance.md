# Baggage Allowance

## Overview

Kenya Airways provides a free checked baggage allowance based on the passenger's travel route, travel class, and ticket type. Baggage allowances shown apply to Kenya Airways operated flights and may differ for codeshare or interline flights operated by partner airlines.

---

# Free Checked Baggage Allowance

### Africa ↔ Europe / America

- **Business Class:** Two checked bags, each weighing up to **32 kg (70 lbs)**.
- **Economy Class:** Two checked bags, each weighing up to **23 kg (50 lbs)**.

### Africa ↔ Middle East / Asia

(Including Bangkok and Guangzhou routes)

- **Business Class:** Two checked bags, each weighing up to **32 kg (70 lbs)**.
- **Economy Class:** Two checked bags, each weighing up to **23 kg (50 lbs)**.

### Bangkok ↔ Guangzhou

- **Business Class:** One checked bag weighing up to **32 kg (70 lbs)**.
- **Economy Class:** One checked bag weighing up to **23 kg (50 lbs)**.

### Nairobi ↔ Mumbai

- **Business Class:** One checked bag weighing up to **32 kg (70 lbs)**.
- **Economy Class:** One checked bag weighing up to **23 kg (50 lbs)**.

### Africa ↔ Africa

- **Business Class:** Two checked bags, each weighing up to **32 kg (70 lbs)**.
- **Economy Class:** Two checked bags, each weighing up to **23 kg (50 lbs)**.

### Domestic Flights

- **Business Class:** One checked bag weighing up to **32 kg (70 lbs)**.
- **Economy Class:** One checked bag weighing up to **23 kg (50 lbs)**.

# Additional Allowance for Asante Rewards Members

Passengers holding the following Asante Rewards membership tiers receive one additional checked baggage piece:

- Asante Silver
- Asante Gold
- Asante Platinum

Additional baggage allowance:

- **Economy Class:** One additional bag up to **23 kg**.
- **Business Class:** One additional bag up to **32 kg**.

---

# Baggage Identification

Each checked baggage item should have a baggage label attached both outside and inside the bag.

The label should include:

- Passenger's full name
- Physical address
- Telephone number

The passenger's name should match the name on the airline ticket.

Providing an email address is also recommended.

---

# Special Allowances

The following items are carried free of charge in addition to the standard baggage allowance:

### Infants

Passengers travelling with an infant may carry:

- One collapsible stroller.
- One child car seat.

### Passengers Requiring Mobility Assistance

Passengers requiring mobility assistance may carry:

- One wheelchair or mobility device free of charge.

### Holy Water

Passengers travelling from Jeddah may carry:

- One sealed bottle of Holy Water.
- Maximum capacity: **5 litres**.

Kenya Airways accepts no liability for Holy Water, and claims cannot be filed for damage or leakage.

---

# Codeshare and Interline Flights

For journeys involving partner airlines:

- Baggage allowance is determined according to **IATA Resolution 302 (Most Significant Carrier Rule)**.
- The baggage allowance displayed on the passenger's ticket, itinerary, or check-in system applies.
- Flights originating from or travelling to the United States or Canada follow the applicable piece concept.

---

# Baggage Pooling

Families or groups travelling together may combine their baggage allowance under the following conditions:

- All passengers must travel together.
- All passengers must be travelling to the same destination.
- All baggage must be checked in together.

Each passenger will still receive an individual baggage claim tag.

**Important:**

Pooling applies only to the **number of baggage pieces**, **not** the weight of each individual bag.

For example:

If two passengers each have an allowance of one **23 kg** bag, they may check two bags in total, but neither bag may exceed **23 kg**.

---

# Important Notes

- Baggage allowances may differ on codeshare or interline flights.
- Sporting equipment and other special baggage may be subject to additional conditions or charges.
- Motorized wheelchairs and other oversized mobility equipment may be accepted under special authorization.
- The baggage allowance shown on the passenger's ticket or in the check-in system takes precedence where applicable.

---

## Excess Baggage

Passengers travelling with baggage exceeding the free baggage allowance are required to pay the applicable excess baggage charges.

For current excess baggage rates and additional information, passengers should contact Kenya Airways Customer Support.
