# Baggage Claim Handling Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure guides customer service agents when handling passenger questions about baggage claims and compensation.

## Procedure

Agents should first:

1. Verify the passenger's baggage reference number.
2. Confirm the type of baggage issue.
3. Check the status of the baggage case.
4. Explain the applicable claim process.
5. Record any additional information provided by the passenger.

## Compensation Requests

Agents should explain that compensation claims are subject to applicable baggage policies and assessment.

Agents must not guarantee compensation before the claim has been reviewed.

## Escalation

Escalate the case when:

- The passenger disputes a claim decision.
- The passenger requests compensation outside the normal process.
- The baggage case involves significant damage or loss.
- Additional authorization is required.

All relevant case details should be recorded before escalation.
