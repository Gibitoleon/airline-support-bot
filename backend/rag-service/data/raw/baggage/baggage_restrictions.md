# Baggage Restrictions

## Overview

Passengers must not pack prohibited or restricted items in either carry-on or checked baggage. These restrictions are intended to ensure the safety and security of passengers, crew members, and aircraft.

### Medical Exception

Medically necessary needles and syringes are permitted when accompanied by prescription medication bearing a professionally printed label identifying the medical office, pharmacy, or medication manufacturer.

---

# Prohibited Items

The following items are prohibited in both checked baggage and carry-on baggage:

- Explosives, munitions, fireworks, and flares.
- Security cases or boxes containing lithium batteries or pyrotechnics.
- Compressed gases (flammable, non-flammable, or poisonous), including butane, propane, scuba cylinders, lighter fuels, and refills.
- Oxidizing substances such as bleaching powder and peroxides.
- Flammable liquids including paints and adhesives.
- Disabling devices such as mace or pepper spray.
- Toxic substances including arsenic, cyanides, and insecticides.
- Radioactive materials.
- Corrosive materials including mercury, acids, alkalis, and wet-cell batteries.
- Magnetized, offensive, irritating, or other hazardous substances that may endanger the aircraft or passengers.
- Lighters (butane, absorbed fuel, electric, battery-powered, and novelty lighters) are prohibited on the person, in carry-on baggage, and in checked baggage for passengers travelling to or from the United States in accordance with Transportation Security Administration (TSA) regulations.

---

# Restricted Items

The following items are not permitted in carry-on baggage but may generally be transported in checked baggage where permitted by applicable regulations:

- Knives, including hunting knives, swords, and pocket knives.
- Scissors and other sharp or bladed objects such as ice picks and nail clippers.
- Weapons including whips, batons, and stun guns.
- Toy guns, replica firearms, gun-shaped items, and handcuffs.
- Sporting equipment including baseball bats, cricket bats, golf clubs, hockey sticks, and billiard cues.
- Devices containing spillable batteries.
- Aerosols such as hair sprays, perfumes, and medicines containing alcohol, provided each container does not exceed **0.5 kg/L** and the total quantity does not exceed **2.0 kg/L**.
- Baby strollers.
- Any item considered a security risk under local laws.
- Any item that resembles or performs a similar function to prohibited or restricted items.

---

# Important Information

Restricted items may be removed by airport security or local authorities at the boarding gate.

Depending on local regulations and airport procedures, confiscated items may not be returned to the passenger.
