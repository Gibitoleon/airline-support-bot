# Delayed, Lost or Damaged Baggage

## Overview

If your checked baggage is delayed, lost, or damaged, you should report the issue immediately upon arrival at the airport. Kenya Airways will create a baggage report, provide a reference number, and assist in tracing your baggage.

---

# Reporting Delayed, Lost or Damaged Baggage

If your baggage is delayed, lost, or damaged:

1. Report the issue at the Kenya Airways baggage service desk immediately after arrival.
2. A baggage report will be created.
3. You will receive a baggage file reference number.
4. Keep the reference number safe for tracking and future claims.

Passengers can use the reference number to track the status of their baggage online or contact Kenya Airways Customer Support for assistance.

---

# Baggage Delivery

If delayed baggage is located, Kenya Airways will arrange delivery to the passenger's local address where this service is available.

Delivery services may not be available in some countries due to local government regulations.

---

# Compensation Claims

Passengers wishing to claim compensation should:

- Submit a written claim within **7 days** of arrival.
- Contact the nearest Kenya Airways office or their travel insurance provider.
- Include the baggage reference number provided when reporting the incident.

Kenya Airways' liability may be reduced if:

- The baggage was not properly packed.
- The baggage was checked in after the recommended check-in time.

---

# First Needs Allowance (FNA)

Passengers whose checked baggage has not arrived within **24 hours** may qualify for a **First Needs Allowance (FNA)** to purchase essential items.

Eligibility depends on Kenya Airways' assessment and applicable conditions.

### General Conditions

- Available only when baggage is delayed for more than 24 hours.
- Passengers must provide receipts for essential purchases.
- Only essential items are eligible for reimbursement.
- The allowance may not apply if at least one checked bag has already been received.
- Residents returning to their country of residence are generally not eligible unless they can demonstrate residence in another country for the previous six months.

### Maximum First Needs Allowance

- First Class / Business Class: **USD 150**
- Economy Class: **USD 100**
- Children aged 2–12 years: **50%** of the applicable allowance.
- Children under 2 years: **Not eligible**.

Any First Needs Allowance paid may be deducted from any final compensation if the baggage is ultimately declared lost.
