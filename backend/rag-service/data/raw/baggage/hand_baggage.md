# Hand Baggage

## Overview

Passengers travelling with Kenya Airways may carry cabin baggage in addition to their checked baggage allowance. Cabin baggage allowances depend on the passenger's travel class and destination.

All cabin baggage must comply with Kenya Airways size and weight requirements and should have an approved Kenya Airways cabin baggage label.

---

# Business Class Cabin Allowance

Passengers travelling in Business Class may carry:

- Two cabin baggage pieces.
- Maximum dimensions for each bag: **55 cm × 25 cm × 35 cm**.
- Combined maximum weight: **18 kg (39 lbs)**.
- One personal accessory.

This allowance applies on:

- Flights to and from the European Union, United States, and United Kingdom.
- All other Kenya Airways routes.

---

# Economy Class Cabin Allowance

## Flights to and from the European Union, United States, and United Kingdom

Passengers may carry:

- One cabin baggage piece.
- Maximum dimensions: **55 cm × 25 cm × 35 cm**.
- Maximum weight: **12 kg (26 lbs)**.
- One personal accessory.

---

## All Other Routes

Passengers may carry:

- One cabin baggage piece.
- Maximum dimensions: **55 cm × 25 cm × 35 cm**.
- Maximum weight: **10 kg (22 lbs)**.
- One personal accessory.

---

# Personal Accessories

Passengers may carry one personal accessory with the following maximum dimensions:

- **40 cm × 15 cm × 30 cm**

Examples include:

- Handbag
- Backpack
- Briefcase
- Camera
- Laptop
- One duty-free shopping bag

---

# Infant Cabin Allowance

Infants under **2 years of age** who do not occupy a separate seat are entitled to:

- One cabin baggage piece.
- Maximum weight: **12 kg**.
- Maximum dimensions: **55 cm × 25 cm × 35 cm**.

If an infant or small child occupies a separate passenger seat, an approved child car seat may be carried on board in addition to the normal cabin baggage allowance.

The accompanying adult is responsible for correctly installing the child car seat according to the manufacturer's instructions.

---

# Additional Information

Passengers may carry **one duty-free shopping bag** in addition to their standard cabin baggage allowance.

Airport authorities or partner airlines may impose additional restrictions on cabin baggage size, weight, or quantity.

If cabin baggage exceeds the permitted allowance but remains within checked baggage limits, it may be checked in free of charge.

If the baggage exceeds the permitted checked baggage allowance, applicable excess baggage charges will apply.

---
## Valuable Items

Passengers should not place valuable items in checked baggage.

Examples include:

- Money
- Jewellery
- Precious metals
- Computers
- Personal electronic devices
- Business documents
- Passports
- Identification documents
- Negotiable papers and securities

Passengers are responsible for keeping these items in their carry-on baggage whenever possible.

## Liability

Kenya Airways is not responsible for loss or damage to prohibited or valuable items placed in checked baggage contrary to its baggage policies.
