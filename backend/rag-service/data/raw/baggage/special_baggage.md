# Special Baggage

## Overview

Special baggage refers to baggage that requires special handling because of its size, weight, nature, or contents. Acceptance is subject to Kenya Airways Conditions of Carriage, aircraft capacity, and applicable safety regulations.

Passengers are advised to contact Kenya Airways before travelling with special baggage, as advance approval may be required.

---

# Bulky Baggage

Bulky baggage includes baggage that:

- Exceeds **32 kg**, or
- Has total dimensions greater than **300 cm (Length + Width + Height)**.

Important information:

- Advance authorization may be required before travel.
- Acceptance depends on aircraft capacity.
- Certain sporting equipment may have additional restrictions.
- Wheelchairs and mobility devices are accepted but should be communicated to the airline before travel.

---

# Fragile and Perishable Items

Kenya Airways accepts fragile baggage, valuable items, and perishable goods at the passenger's own risk.

Examples include:

- Glass items
- Electronics
- Food
- Flowers
- Meat
- Other delicate items

Passengers should:

- Pack fragile items securely.
- Use suitable protective packaging.
- Ensure perishable items are properly sealed.

Items that could leak, spill, or damage other passengers' baggage may not be accepted.

---

# Medicines

Passengers may carry medicines provided they are not classified as Dangerous Goods.

Restricted medicines include those that are:

- Flammable
- Toxic
- Infectious
- Radioactive
- Gas-based

---

# Lithium Batteries

Lithium batteries must be carried safely to reduce the risk of fire.

Requirements include:

- Spare lithium batteries must be carried in carry-on baggage only.
- Spare batteries should be individually protected to prevent short circuits.
- Power banks are treated as spare lithium batteries and must be carried in the cabin.

For baggage containing removable lithium batteries:

- Remove the battery before checking in the baggage, or
- Carry the baggage in the cabin if permitted.

Baggage with non-removable lithium batteries that are designed to charge other devices is not permitted.

---

# Helium Balloons

Helium-filled balloons are not permitted on Kenya Airways flights because they may burst during flight.

---

# Human Remains and Ashes

Human remains must be transported as cargo.

Passengers travelling with the ashes of a deceased person may carry them in the cabin provided that:

- The ashes are contained in a sealed urn.
- The urn fits under the seat or in the overhead compartment.
- The passenger carries the required supporting documents, such as a death certificate or cremation certificate.
- Any destination-specific transport requirements have been satisfied.
