# Book a Flight

## Overview

Passengers can search for and book Kenya Airways flights online to destinations served by the airline. The online booking system allows passengers to select their travel preferences and compare available fares before completing their booking.

---

## How to Book a Flight

To book a flight online:

1. Enter the departure airport or city.
2. Enter the destination airport or city.
3. Select the trip type:
   - One-way
   - Return
   - Multi-city
4. Choose the travel dates.
5. Select the cabin class.
6. Enter the number of passengers.
7. Click **Search Flights** to view available flights.
8. Compare the available fares and select the option that best suits your travel plans.
9. Complete the booking by following the remaining booking and payment steps.

---

## Available Trip Types

- One-way
- Return
- Multi-city

---

## Manage an Existing Booking

Passengers can manage an existing booking through the Kenya Airways **Manage Booking** service.

After logging in with their booking details, passengers can:

- Customize their booking.
- Select or change seats.
- Request special meals.
- Purchase eligible upgrades.

Some changes may be subject to fare conditions and availability.
## Important Notes

- Flight availability depends on the selected travel dates and destination.
- Available fares may vary depending on demand and fare conditions.
- Terms and conditions apply to all bookings.
