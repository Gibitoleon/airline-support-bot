# Customer Service Procedure for Flight Booking Queries

## Purpose

This procedure guides customer service agents when assisting passengers with flight booking enquiries.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the passenger's preferred departure location and destination.
2. Confirm the intended travel dates.
3. Confirm the trip type: one-way, return, or multi-city.
4. Confirm the passenger's preferred travel class.
5. Confirm the number of passengers.
6. Check available flights and fares using the approved booking system.
7. Explain the available options and applicable fare conditions to the passenger.
8. Assist the passenger with the booking process where required.

## Escalation

Escalate the enquiry when:

* No suitable flight is available.
* The booking system shows inconsistent information.
* The passenger requires an exception to standard booking conditions.
* A payment or ticketing issue prevents completion of the booking.

Agents should not guarantee availability or fare prices until the booking is confirmed.
