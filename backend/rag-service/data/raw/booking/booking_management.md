# Customer Service Procedure for Booking Changes and Cancellations

## Purpose

This procedure guides customer service agents when assisting passengers who want to modify or cancel an existing booking.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the passenger's booking reference and identity.
2. Review the existing itinerary and ticket conditions.
3. Determine whether the requested change is permitted under the fare conditions.
4. Check available alternative flights where a date or flight change is requested.
5. Inform the passenger of any applicable fare difference or change charges.
6. Confirm the passenger's approval before processing the change.
7. Provide the updated booking or cancellation confirmation.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

* The ticket conditions cannot be verified.
* The passenger disputes a charge.
* The booking involves another airline.
* A refund requires additional assessment.
* The requested change cannot be processed through the available system.

Agents should not promise a refund or waive applicable charges without authorization.
