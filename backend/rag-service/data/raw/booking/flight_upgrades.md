# Flight Upgrades

## Overview

Passengers holding an Economy Class ticket may upgrade to Business Class through several available options, subject to availability.

## Upgrade Options

### Bid to Upgrade

Passengers can submit a bid for a Business Class upgrade.

- Available to eligible Economy Class passengers.
- Bids can be submitted online up to **7–10 hours before departure**, depending on the departure airport.
- If the bid is successful, the passenger is upgraded to Business Class.

### Buy-Up Upgrade

Passengers may upgrade by paying the fare difference between the Economy Class fare and the available Business Class fare.

- Subject to availability.

### Last-Minute Airport Upgrade

Passengers may request a last-minute upgrade at the airport check-in counter.

- Requires payment of a preset upgrade fee.
- Subject to availability.

### QR Code Upgrade

Passengers may upgrade by scanning the QR code:

- At check-in or transfer desks.
- From the in-flight entertainment screen.
- Subject to availability.

## Benefits of Business Class

Business Class passengers may receive:

- Business Class seating
- Sky Priority check-in, security, and boarding at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport
- Lounge access at stations that offer lounge facilities
- Premium onboard dining
- In-flight entertainment
- Newspapers and magazines

## Baggage

Passengers upgrading through:

- Last-Minute Airport Upgrade
- Bid to Upgrade
- QR Code Upgrade

retain the baggage allowance associated with their original Economy Class ticket.

## Loyalty Points

Tickets upgraded through:

- Last-Minute Airport Upgrade
- Bid to Upgrade
- QR Code Upgrade
- Complimentary upgrades

earn loyalty points based on the original ticket purchased.
