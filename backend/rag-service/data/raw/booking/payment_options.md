# Payment Options

Kenya Airways supports several payment methods for booking flights and purchasing additional services.

## Credit and Debit Cards
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express

After payment is authorized, an e-ticket and payment confirmation are sent by email.

## Mobile Money
Available mobile money payment methods include:
- M-Pesa
- Airtel Money
- Equitel
- Vodacom M-Pesa
- MTN Mobile Money
- Tigo Pesa (Tanzania)

Successful payments generate an e-ticket sent to the passenger's email.

## Digital Wallets and Online Payments
Supported online payment methods include:
- PayPal
- Alipay
- WeChat Pay
- China UnionPay
- iDEAL

## Bank Transfer / Mobile Banking
Bank transfers and mobile banking are available in Kenya, Nigeria, and selected African countries. These provide real-time payments through online banking, mobile apps, and USSD banking.

## Cash Payment
Passengers may reserve a booking online and pay later at selected Kenya Airways ticket offices.

Important:
- Not all ticket offices accept cash.
- Cash payments may attract an additional service fee.
- Passengers should carry their booking confirmation or booking reference when paying.
