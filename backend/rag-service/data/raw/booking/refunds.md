# Refund Policy

Kenya Airways allows refunds for eligible unused or partially used tickets, subject to the fare conditions of the purchased ticket.

## Refund Eligibility

Passengers may be eligible for a refund if:
- The ticket is refundable according to its fare conditions.
- The ticket is unused or partially used.
- The flight was cancelled by Kenya Airways.
- The passenger cancelled an eligible booking.
- Applicable government taxes, airport charges, or fees become refundable.
- The passenger did not travel and is entitled to a refund of applicable taxes and charges, even for certain non-refundable tickets.

## Taxes and Fees

Government taxes, airport charges, and regulatory fees may change after ticket purchase.

- Additional taxes or charges introduced after ticket issuance must be paid.
- If taxes or charges are reduced or abolished, passengers may claim the refundable amount.
- Passengers holding refundable or non-refundable tickets who do not travel may still receive refunds of eligible taxes and charges.

## How to Request a Refund

### Tickets Purchased on kenya-airways.com

Passengers may request refunds through the available refund options.

- If the booking is active and future travel remains, use **Manage My Booking** and select **Cancel and Refund**.
- If travel has already passed or the booking has been cancelled, complete the online refund form.

Choosing to refund a booking cancels the entire ticket and all remaining flight segments.

### Tickets Purchased Through a Travel Agent

Refund requests must be submitted directly to the issuing travel agent.

Kenya Airways cannot process refunds for tickets purchased through travel agencies.

### Tickets Purchased Through Kenya Airways Ticket Offices or Contact Centre

Passengers should visit a Kenya Airways sales office or contact the customer service centre to request cancellation and a refund.

## Refund Processing

- Refunds are returned only to the original payment method.
- Refunds follow the ticket's fare conditions.
- Processing normally takes **21–30 business days** for credit or debit card payments, excluding bank processing time.

## Flight Disruption Refunds

Passengers may request refunds if:

- Kenya Airways cancelled the flight and no alternative flight was accepted.
- The passenger cancelled travel because the flight was delayed by more than eight hours.

These refund options apply only to tickets purchased through kenya-airways.com.

## Medical and Bereavement Refunds

Special refund requests may be submitted for:

### Death of a Passenger

- A full refund is available for the deceased passenger's ticket.
- Family members are not eligible for refunds solely because of the passenger's death.
- Date change fees for accompanying family members may be waived according to policy.

### Medical Reasons

Medical refund requests follow the fare rules.

Free refunds or free ticket changes are not automatically granted for medical cases.

## United States 24-Hour Refund Policy

For flights to or from the United States:

- Bookings made directly through the Kenya Airways website qualify for a full refund if cancelled within **24 hours** of booking.
- The scheduled departure must be at least **7 days** after the booking date.
- Refund requests should be submitted using the online refund form rather than Manage My Booking.
- Refund requests are normally processed within **7 days** after submission.

## Important Notes

- Refund requests are subject to fare rules and applicable penalties.
- Refund requests cannot be submitted by email.
- Once a refund is processed, the itinerary is cancelled.
- Date changes remain subject to the fare conditions of the purchased ticket.
- Passengers generally have **one year from the original ticket issue date** to reschedule travel while retaining the remaining ticket value, less any applicable change fees.
