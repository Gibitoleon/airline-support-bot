# Seat Selection

## Overview

Kenya Airways offers several seat options that allow passengers to customize their travel experience based on their comfort, privacy, and convenience preferences. Seat availability depends on the aircraft type and route.

---

## Neighbour Free Seat

The Neighbour Free Seat option allows passengers to reserve the seat or row next to them to create additional personal space during the flight.

### Benefits

- More personal space.
- Increased privacy.
- Greater comfort throughout the journey.

### Restrictions

Neighbour Free Seats are **not available** for:

- Economy Comfort seats
- Extra Legroom seats
- Exit Row seats

---

## Economy Comfort Seat

Economy Comfort Seats are available on Kenya Airways Dreamliner aircraft and provide additional comfort compared to standard Economy seats.

### Features

- Up to **8 cm of additional legroom**.
- Located near the front of the Economy cabin for faster disembarkation.
- Dedicated overhead cabin baggage storage.
- Designed for improved comfort on longer flights.

---

## Extra Legroom Seats

Extra Legroom Seats provide additional space for passengers who want a more comfortable journey.

### Features

- Additional legroom compared to standard Economy seats.
- Available on Kenya Airways-operated aircraft.
- Suitable for passengers seeking extra comfort during the flight.

---

## Preferred Seats

Preferred Seats are located closer to the front of the Economy cabin.

### Benefits

- Faster disembarkation after landing.
- Convenient cabin location.
- Available on Kenya Airways-operated flights.

---

## Important Notes

- Seat options are subject to availability.
- Available seat types may vary depending on the aircraft and route.
- Additional charges may apply for premium seat selections.
- Passengers can select eligible seats during booking or through the booking management service before departure.
