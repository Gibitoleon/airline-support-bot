# Baggage Drop Off Services

Passengers travelling with checked baggage can use Kenya Airways baggage drop-off services to complete their airport check-in process more efficiently.

Passengers have two options for dropping off their checked baggage:

- Dedicated baggage drop-off counters.
- Standard check-in counters.

Passengers who have completed online check-in and only need to deposit their baggage can use the baggage drop-off counters where available.

## Baggage Drop-Off Process

During baggage drop-off:

1. The passenger presents their baggage at the designated counter.
2. The baggage is weighed to confirm it meets the allowed baggage limits.
3. The baggage is tagged and linked to the passenger's travel information.
4. The baggage is transferred for loading onto the aircraft.

These procedures ensure baggage security and accurate identification throughout the journey.

## Baggage Drop-Off Timing

The required baggage drop-off time depends on the destination.

### Local Flights

Passengers travelling on local flights can drop off their baggage up to 1 hour before the flight closing time.

### International Flights

Passengers travelling internationally should complete baggage drop-off at least 3 hours before departure.

## Passenger Recommendations

Passengers are advised to arrive at the airport early to allow enough time for:

- Baggage drop-off.
- Security screening.
- Immigration procedures.
- Boarding preparation.

Arriving early helps passengers complete airport procedures smoothly and avoid delays.
