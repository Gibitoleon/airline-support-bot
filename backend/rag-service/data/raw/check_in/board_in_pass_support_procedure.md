# Customer Service Procedure for Check-In and Boarding Pass Issues

## Purpose

This procedure guides customer service agents when a passenger cannot complete check-in or obtain a boarding pass.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the passenger's booking reference or ticket number.
2. Verify the passenger's identity and flight details.
3. Determine the reason the boarding pass cannot be generated.
4. Check whether the passenger's booking and travel documents meet the applicable requirements.
5. If online check-in is unavailable, advise the passenger to complete check-in at the airport counter where applicable.
6. If the issue cannot be resolved, direct the passenger to the appropriate airport check-in or customer service team.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

* The passenger's booking details are inconsistent.
* Required travel documents cannot be verified.
* The passenger is unable to check in at the airport.
* The system prevents check-in despite apparently valid booking information.
* The passenger disputes a check-in restriction.

Agents should not manually override check-in restrictions without authorization.
