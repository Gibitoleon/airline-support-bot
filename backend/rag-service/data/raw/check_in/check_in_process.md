# Check-in

## Overview

Kenya Airways offers online, international, domestic, and priority check-in services to help passengers complete the check-in process before departure.

Online check-in allows passengers to save time, select preferred seats, and obtain their boarding pass before arriving at the airport.

---

# Online Check-in

Passengers with an electronic ticket can complete online check-in before arriving at the airport.

## Availability

- International flights: Available from **48 hours before departure** until **1 hour before departure**.
- Flights involving the United States: Available from **24 hours before departure** until **1 hour before departure**.

## Benefits

Passengers using online check-in can:

- Select preferred seats.
- Obtain their boarding pass before arriving at the airport.
- Reduce waiting time at the airport.
- Use designated baggage drop-off counters where available.

After completing online check-in, passengers should proceed to the baggage drop-off counter (if checking baggage) before proceeding to the boarding gate.

---

# International Check-in

Passengers travelling on international flights must:

- Complete check-in at least **2 hours and 30 minutes** before departure.
- Complete check-in regardless of travel class.

Check-in counters close **1 hour before departure**.

---

# Domestic Check-in

Passengers travelling on domestic flights must:

- Present a valid form of identification.
- Complete check-in at least **1 hour and 30 minutes** before departure.

Domestic check-in counters close **30 minutes before departure**.

---

# Priority Check-in

Priority check-in is available to eligible Asante Rewards members travelling from Nairobi.

## Eligibility

Passengers must:

- Hold **Asante Rewards Gold** or **Platinum** status.
- Be travelling on a Kenya Airways flight departing from Nairobi.

## Benefits

Eligible passengers receive:

- Priority check-in
- Priority boarding
- Lounge access with one guest
- Automatic mileage registration when presenting an Asante Rewards card
- Opportunity to request a Business Class upgrade when sufficient miles are available
