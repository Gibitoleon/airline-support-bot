# Customer Service Procedure for Check-In Queries

## Purpose

This procedure guides customer service agents when assisting passengers with check-in enquiries.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the passenger's booking reference and identity.
2. Confirm the passenger's flight date and destination.
3. Determine whether the passenger is attempting online or airport check-in.
4. Confirm that the passenger meets the applicable check-in requirements.
5. Provide the passenger with the appropriate check-in instructions.
6. If the passenger has checked baggage, advise them to proceed to the baggage drop-off counter where available.
7. Remind the passenger of the applicable check-in closing time.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

* The passenger cannot complete check-in despite meeting the requirements.
* Booking or passenger information is incorrect.
* The booking cannot be located.
* The passenger requires an exception to normal check-in procedures.
