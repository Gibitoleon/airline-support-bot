# Self-Service Airport Check-In

Kenya Airways provides self-service airport check-in kiosks that allow passengers to complete the check-in process conveniently before their flight.

Self-service kiosks help passengers verify their travel details, generate boarding passes, and complete required airport procedures.

## Self-Service Check-In Process

### 1. Arrival at the Check-In Kiosk

Passengers should locate the self-service check-in kiosks available at the airport.

The kiosks are designed to provide a faster and more convenient check-in experience.

### 2. Select Airline

Passengers select their airline from the list displayed on the kiosk.

Available airlines may include Kenya Airways and partner airlines.

### 3. Dangerous Goods Declaration

The kiosk displays information about dangerous goods and restricted items that cannot be carried on board an aircraft.

Passengers must declare any dangerous goods where applicable to comply with aviation safety regulations.

### 4. Passenger Identification

Passengers verify their identity using available travel information, including:

- Booking reference number.
- Ticket number.
- Frequent flyer details.
- Passport information.

### 5. Booking Verification

After successful identification, the kiosk retrieves the passenger's booking details.

Passengers should confirm that their information is correct, including:

- Contact details.
- Flight information.
- Passenger details.

### 6. Boarding Pass Generation

After confirming the travel information, the kiosk generates the passenger's boarding pass.

The boarding pass contains important flight details such as:

- Departure gate.
- Seat number.
- Departure time.

Passengers may choose to:

- Print the boarding pass at the kiosk.
- Receive a digital boarding pass through email or mobile messaging.

### 7. Completing Check-In

Once the boarding pass has been generated, the self-service check-in process is complete.

Passengers can proceed to:

- Security screening.
- Immigration procedures where applicable.
- Departure gates.

## Benefits of Self-Service Check-In

Self-service check-in allows passengers to:

- Complete check-in faster.
- Manage their travel details independently.
- Reduce waiting time at traditional check-in counters.
- Receive their boarding pass conveniently.
