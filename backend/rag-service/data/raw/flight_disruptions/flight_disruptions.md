# Flight Delays and Cancellations

## Overview

Kenya Airways may experience flight delays or cancellations due to operational factors, including:

- Weather conditions
- Air traffic control restrictions
- Technical challenges

Passengers whose flights are delayed, cancelled, or rescheduled may use the Kenya Airways Self-Rebooking Service to change their flight online at no additional cost.

---

# Self-Rebooking Service

The Kenya Airways Self-Rebooking Service enables eligible passengers to rebook disrupted flights through the Kenya Airways website or mobile application without contacting customer support.

## Benefits

- Free rebooking with no penalties
- Ability to choose an alternative flight
- Saves time by avoiding customer support waiting times
- Convenient online self-service
- Multiple flight options based on availability

---

# How to Rebook

1. Visit the Kenya Airways Self-Rebooking portal.
2. Enter your booking reference or electronic ticket number together with your last name.
3. View the available alternative flights.
4. Select the preferred flight.
5. Confirm your new booking.
6. Receive an updated electronic ticket and confirmation email.

---

# Eligibility

The Self-Rebooking Service is available only for flights that have experienced:

- Flight delays
- Flight cancellations
- Schedule changes

Flights that have not been disrupted are not eligible for self-rebooking.

---

# Available Flight Options

Passengers may choose from the available alternative flights offered by the system.

Rebooking options generally include flights that depart:

- Within 24 hours of the original flight, or
- Up to 7 days after the original scheduled departure.

---

# Existing Booking Details

The following booking details remain unchanged after self-rebooking:

- Destination
- Travel class
- Baggage allowance
- Previously selected services

---

# Automatic Rebooking

Passengers who do not complete the self-rebooking process will automatically be rebooked onto the next available flight.

If satisfied with the automatically assigned flight, passengers should accept the proposed changes.

Passengers wishing to choose another available option should access the **Manage Booking** portal.

---

# Checked Baggage

If baggage has already been checked in before the disruption, it will automatically be transferred to the newly selected flight.

---

# Charges

The Self-Rebooking Service is free of charge for eligible disrupted flights.

---

# Ticket Confirmation

After confirming the selected flight, passengers receive:

- An updated electronic ticket
- Email confirmation

---

# Supported Ticket Types

The Self-Rebooking Service supports:

- Revenue tickets
- Award tickets
- Eligible partner airline tickets containing Kenya Airways-operated flight sectors

Partner airline tickets may require ticket reissuance instead of ticket revalidation.

---

# Multiple Flight Disruptions

Passengers may use the Self-Rebooking Service once for each separate flight disruption.

---

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## What is the Kenya Airways Self-Rebooking Service?

The Self-Rebooking Service allows passengers whose flights have been delayed, cancelled, or rescheduled to rebook themselves online without contacting customer support.

---

## Will I be charged to use the Self-Rebooking Service?

No. The Self-Rebooking Service is provided free of charge.

---

## Will my flight preferences and selected services remain the same?

Yes. The following remain unchanged:

- Destination
- Travel class
- Baggage allowance
- Previously selected services

---

## What do I need to rebook my flight?

You need:

- Your booking reference or electronic ticket number.
- Your last name.

---

## Where do I rebook my flight?

Visit the Kenya Airways website and select **Manage Booking**.

---

## Where can I see the available flight options?

After logging into the **Manage Booking** portal, the system displays all available alternative flights.

Available options generally include flights:

- Within 24 hours of the original flight.
- Up to 7 days after the original scheduled departure.

---

## Can I rebook a flight that has not been delayed or cancelled?

No. Self-Rebooking is available only for flights affected by delays, cancellations, or schedule changes.

---

## What happens if I do not rebook my flight?

The system automatically rebooks you onto the next available flight.

If you are satisfied with the new itinerary, accept the proposed changes.

If you would like another available option, access **Manage Booking** to choose your preferred flight.

Do not select **Decline Changes**, as this redirects the booking to Kenya Airways Customer Support for manual assistance.

---

## Will I receive a new electronic ticket and boarding pass?

Yes.

After confirming your new flight you will receive:

- Email confirmation
- Updated electronic ticket

---

## Will I be charged for rebooking?

No.

Passengers using the Self-Rebooking Service for eligible disrupted flights are not charged any rebooking fee.

---

## If I have already checked in, will my baggage be transferred automatically?

Yes.

Checked baggage is automatically transferred to the newly selected flight.

---

## If my outbound flight changes, can I also change my inbound flight free of charge?

No.

The Self-Rebooking Service only applies to the disrupted flight segment.

If changes to an unaffected inbound flight are required, passengers should contact their travel agent or Kenya Airways Customer Support.

---

## Can the Self-Rebooking Service rebook partner airline tickets?

Yes.

Eligible partner airline tickets that include Kenya Airways-operated flight sectors can be rebooked.

Such tickets may require ticket reissuance instead of ticket revalidation.

---

## Can award tickets be rebooked?

Yes.

Award tickets are eligible for Self-Rebooking.

---

## What happens if my flight is disrupted more than once?

Passengers may use the Self-Rebooking Service once for each separate disruption.

---
