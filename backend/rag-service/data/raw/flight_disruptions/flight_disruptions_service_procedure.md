# Customer Service Procedure for Flight Disruptions

## Purpose

This procedure guides customer service agents when assisting passengers affected by flight delays, cancellations, or schedule changes.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the passenger's booking reference and affected flight.
2. Verify the current disruption information using the approved airline system.
3. Explain the available rebooking options to the passenger.
4. Assist the passenger with an eligible alternative flight where applicable.
5. Confirm the updated itinerary and provide the passenger with the new booking information.
6. If the passenger requires a refund or another resolution, explain the applicable process.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

* No suitable alternative flight is available.
* The passenger requests compensation or an exception.
* The booking involves another airline.
* The disruption cannot be resolved through the available rebooking system.

Agents should not promise compensation or waive applicable charges without authorization.
