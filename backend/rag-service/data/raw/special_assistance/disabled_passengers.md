# Passengers with Reduced Mobility

## Overview

Passengers with reduced mobility are passengers with medical or neurodevelopmental disabilities who require additional assistance beyond the support normally provided during:

- Boarding and disembarkation.
- Ground handling.
- Flight operations.
- Emergency evacuation situations.

Kenya Airways provides assistance services to support passengers with reduced mobility throughout their journey.

---

# Wheelchair Assistance

Wheelchair assistance is provided free of charge.

Passengers requiring wheelchair assistance should contact Kenya Airways through available customer support channels to request and reserve the service.

---

# Requesting Wheelchair Assistance

Passengers travelling to or from:

- London Heathrow (LHR).
- London Gatwick (LGW).
- Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG).

should request wheelchair assistance at least **48 hours before departure**.

Requests made less than 48 hours before departure are subject to airport operational availability and cannot be guaranteed.

---

# Bringing Personal Wheelchairs and Mobility Devices

Passengers may bring their own wheelchair or mobility device free of charge.

Passengers should make a reservation for their mobility equipment at least **48 hours before departure**.

---

# Wheelchair Transportation

## Cabin or Hand Baggage

Collapsible or dismountable manual wheelchairs may be accepted as cabin baggage if they meet aircraft size restrictions.

## Checked Baggage

Other types of wheelchairs and mobility devices are transported as checked baggage.

Passengers may use airport-provided wheelchairs after checking in.

---

- Height: 149 cm

- No maximum weight specified.

- No maximum weight specified.

- Width: 100 cm

# Passengers with Reduced Mobility Travelling Alone

Passengers may travel alone if they are able to independently support themselves onboard and assist with their own evacuation if required.

A passenger travelling alone must be able to:

- Eat without assistance.
- Take medication without assistance.
- Use the lavatory without assistance.

Cabin crew assistance is limited to providing and moving an onboard wheelchair to and from the lavatory entrance.

Passengers who cannot perform these activities independently must travel with a companion or personal care attendant.

---

# Groups of Passengers with Reduced Mobility

Groups of five or more passengers with reduced mobility require advance arrangements.

Additional:

- Ground equipment.
- Assistance staff.
- Support reorigins.

may be arranged depending on operational requirements.

Groups should provide able-bodied attendants where required.

General requirements:

- Persons below 18 years old should not act as attendants.
- The number of attendants required depends on the passengers' ability to reach aircraft exits during emergencies.
- Escort personnel receive instructions regarding emergency procedures.

---

# Important Notes

- Wheelchair assistance must be requested before travel.
- Passengers should provide details about their mobility requirements during booking.
- Availability of certain services may depend on aircraft type and airport facilities.
