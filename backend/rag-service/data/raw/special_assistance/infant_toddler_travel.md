# Infant and Toddler Travel

## Overview

Kenya Airways provides services and facilities to support families travelling with infants and toddlers, helping ensure a safe and comfortable journey.

---

# Boarding and Seating

Families travelling with infants may receive:

- Priority boarding to allow additional time for boarding and settling in.
- Access to bulkhead seating, where available.
- Bassinet-equipped seats on eligible aircraft.

Passengers are encouraged to request a bassinet during booking or by contacting Kenya Airways before travel. Availability is subject to aircraft type and seat availability.

---

# In-Flight Facilities

To assist families travelling with infants, Kenya Airways provides:

- Changing tables in aircraft lavatories.
- Warm water for preparing baby bottles.
- Limited emergency baby supplies, including diapers and wipes, when available.

---

# Cabin Crew Assistance

Cabin crew are available to assist with:

- Bottle warming and preparation.
- Stroller storage.
- Guidance on infant travel during the flight.
- Infant-friendly entertainment where available.

---

# Meals

Special baby meals are available upon request for infants who have started eating solid food.

Passengers should request these meals before departure to ensure availability.

---

# Lap Infant Policy

Passengers travelling with lap infants should review Kenya Airways policies regarding:

- Age restrictions.
- Ticketing requirements.
- Applicable baggage allowance.

---

# Comfort During the Flight

Parents and guardians may help reduce ear discomfort during takeoff and landing by:

- Feeding the infant.
- Offering a pacifier.

Cabin crew can provide guidance when needed.

---

# Safety

The safety of infants remains a priority throughout the journey.

Cabin crew will provide guidance on:

- Securing infants during turbulence.
- Following onboard infant safety procedures.

---

# Important Notes

- Bassinets are available only on selected aircraft and are subject to availability.
- Baby meals should be requested before travel.
- Cabin crew are available to assist families throughout the journey.
- Passengers travelling with lap infants should review the applicable Kenya Airways travel policies before departure.
