# Medical and Special Needs Assistance

## Overview

Kenya Airways provides medical and special assistance services to passengers who require additional support due to medical conditions, disabilities, or special travel requirements.

Passengers with medical conditions are advised to declare their condition before travel so that appropriate assistance and safety arrangements can be made.

Passengers are also encouraged to obtain travel insurance, as medical costs may vary depending on the destination country.

---

# Medical Clearance Requirements

Passengers with certain medical conditions require medical clearance before travelling.

Medical clearance may be required if a passenger:

- Recently experienced a major medical condition such as heart attack, heart failure, stroke, respiratory failure, or pneumothorax.
- Had surgery within the previous two weeks.
- Has a pre-existing heart or respiratory condition.
- Has a medical condition that may be affected by air travel.
- Has thrombophlebitis or a history of pulmonary embolism.
- Has unstable mental illness or impairment.
- Cannot care for themselves and requires special assistance.
- Cannot use a normal upright aircraft seat and requires a stretcher or additional seating arrangements.
- Requires in-flight oxygen, a portable oxygen concentrator (POC), or a ventilator.
- Requires battery-powered medical equipment or medical procedures during the flight.

Passengers with any of these conditions should complete a Medical Information Form for Fitness to Fly (MEDIF) before travel.

---

# Medical Clearance Process

Passengers requiring medical clearance must:

1. Complete the MEDIF (Medical Information Form for Fitness to Fly).
2. Submit the completed form and any required medical reports before travel.
3. Receive medical approval before flying.

Once approved, Kenya Airways provides a Passenger Medical Clearance (PMC) form stating the conditions of travel clearance.

Passengers must carry the PMC form throughout their journey.

---

# MEDIF Requirements

The MEDIF form and supporting medical reports must:

- Be completed in English.
- Be fully completed, including the passenger declaration section.
- Be signed, stamped, and dated clearly.

Important requirements:

- An incomplete MEDIF may delay the clearance process.
- A new MEDIF may be required for return journeys.
- Passengers must notify Kenya Airways medical services if their medical condition changes before travel.
- A new MEDIF may be required if the condition changes significantly.

---

# Ground Medical Support

Passengers requiring medical support services before, during, or after travel must make arrangements in advance.

Examples of support services include:

- Ambulance services.
- Airport medical assistance.
- Oxygen arrangements.
- Hospital or pre-hospital support.
- Connecting flight assistance.

Additional facilitation fees may apply.

Medical equipment required during the flight must receive approval from Kenya Airways Engineering at least **24 hours before departure**.

---

# Medical Devices

Passengers travelling with medical devices should notify Kenya Airways medical services before travel.

Requirements for medical devices:

- Devices used onboard must comply with applicable IATA standards.
- Details of the medical device should be provided before travel.
- Battery-powered devices must use dry-cell batteries unless otherwise approved.
- Batteries must provide sufficient power for at least **1.5 times the flight duration**.
- Batteries must be transported according to applicable safety regulations.

---

# Portable Oxygen Concentrators (POC)

Passengers carrying portable oxygen concentrators (POC):

- Must obtain approval before travel.
- Must remove the battery if the device is carried as cabin baggage but will not be used during the flight.
- Must package removed batteries separately unless the device has protective features preventing accidental activation.

---

# Ventilators and Medical Equipment

Passengers requiring ventilators:

- Must submit a MEDIF form.
- Require approval on a case-by-case basis.
- May require an accompanying medical escort experienced in aeromedical transportation.

---

# Medication on Board

Passengers travelling with medication should:

- Carry prescriptions and doctor's letters in their cabin baggage.
- Check medication transportation requirements for their destination country.
- Inform cabin crew if medication injections need to be administered during the flight.

Important:

- Medication cannot be refrigerated onboard the aircraft.

---

# Oxygen Assistance

Kenya Airways provides medical oxygen for passengers who require it, subject to medical clearance.

Passengers requiring oxygen during the flight may require:

- Medical approval.
- An appropriate medical escort, depending on the situation.

---

# Stretcher Services

Kenya Airways provides stretcher services for passengers requiring additional medical support.

Requirements:

- Stretcher services require medical clearance.
- Requests must be made at least **48 hours before departure**.
- Passengers may require a medical certificate.
- Passengers requiring stretcher services must travel with an appropriate medical escort.

---

# Medical Escort Services

Passengers requiring medical support during travel, including those:

- Travelling on a stretcher.
- Requiring prescribed oxygen.
- Requiring specialised medical assistance.

should notify Kenya Airways medical services at least **48 hours before departure**.

Medical escort services are available at an additional fee.

---

# Medical Facilitation Fees

Medical services may involve additional charges depending on the service required.

Examples include:

- Airport medical review.
- Passenger medical clearance.
- Ambulance services.
- Oxygen supply.
- Stretcher services.
- Portable oxygen concentrator arrangements.

Applicable fees depend on the route and service requirements.

---

# Service Animals

Kenya Airways allows trained and certified service dogs to assist passengers with disabilities.

Service animals include dogs individually trained to perform tasks for passengers with:

- Physical disabilities.
- Sensory disabilities.
- Psychiatric disabilities.
- Intellectual disabilities.
- Other qualifying conditions.

Emotional support animals, comfort animals, companion animals, and animals in training are not considered service animals unless they meet applicable requirements.

---

# Service Animal Booking Requirements

Passengers travelling with service dogs must:

- Make an advance reservation up to **48 hours before departure**.
- Provide required documentation.
- Contact Kenya Airways medical services or customer support before travel.

For flights to and from the United States, passengers must provide required U.S. Department of Transportation service animal forms.

---

# Service Animal Conditions

Service animals must:

- Remain with the passenger throughout the journey.
- Wear a harness.
- Behave appropriately in public areas.

Additional requirements:

- Maximum of one service dog per passenger.
- Maximum of one service dog on narrow-body aircraft.
- Maximum of two service dogs on wide-body aircraft.
- Service dogs must generally be older than four months.
- Service animals cannot occupy emergency exit seats.

---

# Service Animal Documentation

Required documentation may include:

- Kenya Airways approved medical forms.
- Medical documentation where applicable.
- Dog health certificate.
- Import/export documents.
- Veterinary documentation.
- Dog training certificate.

Documentation should generally not be older than one year from the departure date.

---

# Important Notes

- Passengers requiring medical assistance should declare their needs before travel.
- Medical clearance requirements depend on the passenger's condition and travel requirements.
- MEDIF approval may be required before flying.
- Medical equipment and oxygen arrangements require prior approval.
- Advance arrangements are required for specialised assistance services.
- Travel requirements may differ depending on destination country regulations.
