# Expectant Mothers Travel Guidelines

## Overview

Kenya Airways provides travel guidelines for expectant mothers to support safe and comfortable journeys during pregnancy.

Pregnant passengers are advised to consult their healthcare provider before travelling, especially during the later stages of pregnancy or if they have existing medical conditions.

---

# Medical Documentation Requirements

Depending on the stage of pregnancy and medical circumstances, passengers may be required to provide medical documentation confirming their fitness to travel.

A doctor's letter may assist Kenya Airways' medical team in assessing a passenger's fitness for air travel but does not replace a required clearance certificate.

For flights to and from the United States, medical letters should be dated within **10 days of departure**.

Passengers should carry any required medical documentation during their journey.

---

# Pregnancy Travel Restrictions

## Single Uncomplicated Pregnancy

Passengers with a single uncomplicated pregnancy should follow these requirements:

- A clearance certificate from a Kenya Airways doctor is required after **28 weeks of pregnancy**.
- Passengers will not be accepted for travel after reaching **36 weeks of pregnancy**.

---

## Multiple Uncomplicated Pregnancy

Passengers with multiple uncomplicated pregnancies should follow these requirements:

- A clearance certificate from a Kenya Airways doctor is required after **28 weeks of pregnancy**.
- Passengers will not be accepted for travel after reaching **32 weeks of pregnancy**.

---

## Complicated Pregnancy

Passengers with complicated pregnancies require a clearance certificate from a Kenya Airways doctor before travelling.

Travel approval depends on medical assessment and fitness to fly.

---

# Pregnancy Travel Preparation

Expectant passengers are advised to prepare for their journey by:

- Consulting their healthcare provider before flying.
- Staying hydrated throughout the flight.
- Carrying necessary medications and personal items in their carry-on baggage.
- Carrying a change of clothes and other essential items.
- Wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing and supportive footwear.
- Allowing enough time for airport procedures to reduce stress.
- Considering travel insurance that covers medical emergencies.

---

# Seat Selection and Comfort

Passengers may improve their comfort during travel by:

- Selecting seats with additional space where available.
- Adjusting their seating position for comfort.
- Using available pillows for support.

---

# In-Flight Safety and Assistance

During the flight, expectant passengers are encouraged to:

- Move around the cabin when possible to promote circulation.
- Perform simple stretches during the journey.
- Keep their seatbelt fastened while seated.

Cabin crew are available to assist passengers who experience discomfort during the flight.

---

# Destination Planning

Expectant passengers should consider medical preparedness at their destination by:

- Researching available medical facilities.
- Understanding emergency support options.
- Planning their journey according to their health requirements.

---

# Important Notes

- Pregnancy travel acceptance depends on pregnancy stage and medical condition.
- Medical clearance requirements apply after **28 weeks** of pregnancy.
- Single pregnancies are not accepted for travel after **36 weeks**.
- Multiple pregnancies are not accepted for travel after **32 weeks**.
- Complicated pregnancies require medical clearance before travel.
- Passengers should carry all required medical documents during their journey.
