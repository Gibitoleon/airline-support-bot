# Unaccompanied Minor Service

## Overview

Kenya Airways provides an Unaccompanied Minor (UM) service for children travelling alone on Kenya Airways-operated flights. The service ensures children receive supervision and assistance throughout their journey.

---

# Eligibility

- Available for children aged **5 to 14 years** travelling alone.
- Children **below 5 years** must travel with an adult guardian.
- Young adults aged **15 to 17 years** may use the service upon request from a parent or guardian.
- The service is available **only on Kenya Airways-operated flights**.

---

# Required Documentation

Passengers using the Unaccompanied Minor service must:

- Complete the **Unaccompanied Minor Form** accurately.
- Provide details of:
  - The child.
  - Parent(s) or guardian(s).
  - The designated person collecting the child at the destination.

---

# Fees

- An Unaccompanied Minor service fee applies.
- The fee includes supervision and assistance throughout the journey.
- The fee is **waived for domestic travel** on:
  - Nairobi (NBO)
  - Mombasa (MBA)
  - Kisumu (KIS)

For groups of unaccompanied minors:

- If **no adult accompanies the group**, Unaccompanied Minor fees apply.
- If **an adult accompanies the group**, the standard group travel policy applies.

---

# Travel Guidelines

To ensure a safe journey:

- Non-stop or minimally connected flights are recommended.
- Overnight flight connections are **not permitted**.
- Early check-in is recommended to allow document verification.

For itineraries involving another airline:

- The Unaccompanied Minor service ends at the international airport if the onward journey is operated by a different airline.

---

# Airport and In-Flight Assistance

Kenya Airways provides:

- Escort assistance through airport security and to the boarding gate.
- Supervision by cabin crew during the flight.
- Assistance with meals, entertainment, and passenger needs.
- Special meals when requested in advance.

---

# Pick-up Requirements

The designated person collecting the child should:

- Arrive before the scheduled arrival time.
- Be available to receive the child promptly upon arrival.

Children should also have contact information for their parents or guardians and the designated pick-up person.

---

# Special Programs

Students travelling under the **KOOLFLYERS** program must present a valid membership card when using the Unaccompanied Minor service.

---

# Service Disruptions

If unexpected travel disruptions occur:

- Kenya Airways follows established procedures to ensure the child's safety.
- Parents, guardians, and the designated pick-up person are kept informed throughout the journey.

---

# Important Notes

- The service is available only on Kenya Airways-operated flights.
- Children under **5 years** cannot travel unaccompanied.
- Overnight flight connections are not allowed.
- An accurately completed Unaccompanied Minor Form is required before travel.
