# Class of Service

## Overview

Kenya Airways provides different classes of service designed to meet passenger comfort, convenience, and travel preferences. Available services include Business Class, Economy Class, Economy Comfort Seats, and Priority Travel services.

---

# Business Class (Premier World)

Kenya Airways Business Class, also known as Premier World, provides an enhanced travel experience focused on comfort, relaxation, and premium services.

Business Class passengers receive:

- Spacious seating designed for increased comfort.
- A premium cabin environment suitable for both short-haul and long-haul journeys.
- Enhanced onboard service and hospitality.
- Premium meals and amenities during the flight.
- A more comfortable travel experience for passengers seeking additional relaxation.

Business Class is designed for passengers who value additional comfort, privacy, and premium services during their journey.

---

# Economy Class

Kenya Airways Economy Class provides passengers with comfortable seating and onboard services while maintaining affordable travel options.

Economy Class passengers receive:

- Comfortable seating within the economy cabin.
- Onboard hospitality services.
- Meal services depending on the flight duration and route.
- Access to available onboard entertainment services.

Economy Class is suitable for passengers looking for reliable air travel with essential onboard services.

---

# Economy Comfort Seats

Economy Comfort Seats provide additional space and comfort compared to standard Economy Class seats.

Passengers who reserve Economy Comfort Seats receive:

- Additional legroom.
- Increased personal space.
- An additional armrest for improved comfort.
- A more relaxed seating experience compared to standard economy seating.

Economy Comfort Seats are available for passengers who want extra comfort while travelling in Economy Class.

---

# Priority Travel Services

Kenya Airways offers Priority Travel services for passengers seeking faster and more convenient airport experiences.

Priority Travel benefits may include:

- Priority check-in.
- Priority security clearance where available.
- Priority boarding.
- Priority baggage delivery.
- Priority arrival services.
- Access to Kenya Airways lounges including Pride and Simba lounges for eligible passengers.

Priority Travel services help passengers save time and enjoy a smoother airport experience.

---

# Common Passenger Questions

## What is the difference between Business Class and Economy Class?

Business Class provides additional comfort, premium services, and enhanced seating compared to Economy Class, which provides essential onboard services at a more affordable travel option.

## Does Kenya Airways have extra legroom seats?

Yes. Economy Comfort Seats provide passengers with additional legroom and increased seating space for improved comfort.

## What benefits come with Priority Travel?

Priority Travel may include priority check-in, boarding, baggage handling, security services, and lounge access for eligible passengers.
