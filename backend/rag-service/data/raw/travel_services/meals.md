# Special Meals and Onboard Dining

## Overview

Kenya Airways provides onboard meal services for passengers depending on travel class, flight duration, and individual dietary requirements.

Passengers can request special meals based on medical, religious, dietary, or personal preferences before travelling.

---

# Meals in Business Class

Kenya Airways Business Class passengers receive meal services depending on the duration and timing of their flight.

## Short Haul Flights

For flights between 46 minutes and 1 hour 30 minutes, passengers are served freshly prepared cold and hot snacks with a choice of beverage.

## Medium Haul Flights

For flights between 1 hour 46 minutes and 5 hours, passengers receive a hot light meal consisting of a main course, dessert, bread, and butter.

Depending on the departure time, passengers may also receive a hot breakfast or brunch.

## Long Haul Flights

For flights between 6 hours and 11 hours, passengers receive two meal services depending on the flight duration and time of day.

Meals are prepared and served fresh by the cabin crew.

## Super Long Haul Flights

For flights longer than 11 hours, passengers receive three meal services depending on the flight duration and time of day.

Meals are prepared and served fresh by the cabin crew.

---

# Meals in Economy Class

Kenya Airways Economy Class meal services depend on flight duration.

## Short Haul Flights

For flights between 46 minutes and 1 hour 30 minutes, passengers receive freshly prepared cold and hot snacks with a beverage of choice.

## Medium Haul Flights

For flights between 1 hour 46 minutes and 5 hours, passengers receive a hot light meal, hot breakfast, or continental breakfast depending on the time of day.

## Long Haul Flights

For flights between 6 hours and 11 hours, passengers receive two meal services.

Meal options may include lunch, dinner, or hot breakfast depending on the flight schedule.

## Super Long Haul Flights

For flights longer than 11 hours, passengers receive three meal services depending on the flight schedule.

---

# Special Meal Requests

Kenya Airways provides special meals to accommodate passengers with different dietary, religious, medical, and lifestyle requirements.

Passengers should request special meals before travelling.

Special meals include:

- Medical meals.
- Religious meals.
- Vegetarian meals.
- Baby and child meals.
- Other special meal requests.

---

# Religious Meals

## Hindu Meal (HNML)

Hindu meals are prepared for passengers following Hindu dietary practices.

Meals may contain ingredients such as lamb, poultry, fish, milk products, spicy foods, and curry.

## Kosher Meal (KSML)

Kosher meals are prepared according to Jewish dietary requirements.

Passengers should request Kosher meals at least 48 hours before departure.

Availability may vary depending on the flight.

## Moslem Meal (MOML)

Moslem meals are prepared according to Islamic dietary requirements.

Meals may contain halal-certified meat or poultry from approved origins.

## Jain Meal (VJML)

Jain meals are vegetarian meals prepared according to Jain dietary customs.

They do not contain:

- Meat.
- Poultry.
- Fish.
- Seafood.
- Eggs.
- Root or bulb vegetables.
- Dairy products.

Meals may include fruits, vegetables, spices, tofu, pulses, cereals, and rice.

---

# Vegetarian Meals

## Asian Vegetarian Meal (AVML)

A vegetarian meal prepared in an Asian style.

It may contain:

- Dairy products.
- Lentils.
- Beans.
- Tofu.
- Vegetables.
- Spices.

## Vegetarian Vegan Meal (VGML)

A vegan meal that does not contain animal products or animal by-products.

It may contain:

- Lentils.
- Beans.
- Tofu.
- Vegetables.
- Whole grains.

## Vegetarian Raw Meal (RVML)

A meal consisting mainly of raw fruits and vegetables.

## Lacto-Ovo Vegetarian Meal (VLML)

A vegetarian meal that may contain:

- Milk products.
- Cheese.
- Yogurt.
- Eggs.
- Lentils.
- Beans.
- Tofu.

## Vegetarian Oriental Meal (VOML)

A vegetarian meal prepared in an oriental style containing vegetables, fruits, and spices.

---

# Medical and Dietary Meals

## Gluten Intolerant Meal (GFML)

Designed for passengers who are allergic or intolerant to gluten.

The meal avoids ingredients containing wheat, barley, oats, or rye.

## Bland Meal (BLML)

Designed for passengers requiring easily digestible food.

It may include:

- Mashed potatoes.
- Vegetables.
- Soft-boiled eggs.
- Boiled meats.
- Toast.
- Dairy products.

## Diabetic Meal (DBML)

A low-sugar meal designed for passengers with diabetes.

It may include:

- Lean meat.
- Fish.
- Poultry.
- Vegetables.
- Whole grains.
- High-fiber foods.

## Low Fat Meal (LFML)

Designed for passengers following a low-fat diet.

It may include:

- Lean meats.
- Fruits.
- Vegetables.
- Whole grain products.
- Low-fat dairy products.

## Low Lactose Meal (NLML)

Designed for passengers who are allergic or intolerant to lactose.

The meal avoids dairy products and may contain lactose-free alternatives.

## Low Salt Meal (LSML)

Designed for passengers requiring reduced sodium intake.

---

# Baby and Child Meals

## Baby Meal (BBML)

Baby meals are available for infants up to two years old.

They may include:

- Strained fruits.
- Strained vegetables.
- Strained meats.
- Desserts.

Parents are encouraged to bring familiar food for infants.

Cabin crew can assist with warming baby meals and providing bottled water.

## Child Meal (CHML)

Child meals are available for children between 2 and 12 years old.

Meals are designed to be soft, easy to chew, and suitable for children.

Child meals are available on flights longer than four hours.

---

# Other Special Meals

## Fruit Platter (FPML)

A fresh fruit meal containing seasonal fruits without additives or preservatives.

## Seafood Meal (SFML)

A meal prepared according to passenger preference containing fish or seafood.

## Special Meal (SPML)

A special meal request that does not fall under a specific meal category.

---

# Nut Allergies

Kenya Airways cannot guarantee a completely nut-free environment.

Nuts may be:

- Served as snacks.
- Used as ingredients in onboard meals.
- Brought onboard by other passengers.

Passengers with nut allergies should consult their doctor before travelling and may consider bringing their own meals.

---

# Requesting Special Meals

Passengers should request special meals at least 24 hours before flight departure.

Special meals can be requested through:

- Kenya Airways website using the Book and Manage feature.
- Kenya Airways 24-hour contact centre.
- Kenya Airways sales offices.
- Travel agencies.
