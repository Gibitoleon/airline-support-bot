# Customer Service Procedure for Travel Service Requests

## Purpose

This procedure guides customer service agents when passengers request additional travel services.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the passenger's booking and identity.
2. Identify the requested service, such as seat selection, special meals, or lounge access.
3. Check whether the service is available for the passenger's booking and route.
4. Inform the passenger of any applicable conditions or charges.
5. Add the requested service through the approved system where permitted.
6. Confirm the service has been successfully added to the booking.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

* The requested service is unavailable.
* The service requires special authorization.
* The system cannot add the requested service.
* The passenger disputes an applicable charge.
